Himalayan Region

The Himalayan Region lies in the northern part of Nepal. It is famous for snow-covered mountains, cold climate, glaciers, and some of the highest peaks in the world, including Mount Everest.

This region attracts trekkers, climbers, and tourists from all around the world. People here often follow mountain lifestyles, and tourism is an important part of the economy.

Hilly Region

The Hilly Region is located between the Himalayas and the Terai. It has beautiful valleys, forests, rivers, and terraced farms. Kathmandu and Pokhara are two famous cities in this region.

This region is known for its cultural diversity, traditional villages, moderate climate, and scenic landscapes.

Terai Region

The Terai Region is the southern lowland area of Nepal. It has flat land, fertile soil, warmer temperatures, and is very important for farming.

This region produces many crops such as rice, wheat, sugarcane, and vegetables. It is also home to national parks, wildlife, and many growing cities.
