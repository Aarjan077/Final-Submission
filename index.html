Tourism

Nepal is one of the most beautiful tourism destinations in the world. It is famous for Mount Everest, trekking routes, temples, lakes, rivers, forests, wildlife, and rich culture. Tourists visit Nepal for adventure, nature, history, and spiritual experiences. Popular places include Kathmandu Valley, Pokhara, Lumbini, Chitwan National Park, and the Everest region.

Regions

Nepal has three main geographical regions. The Himalayan Region has snow-covered mountains and cold climate. The Hilly Region has valleys, forests, rivers, and terraced farms. The Terai Region has flat, fertile land and warmer weather. Each region has its own culture, lifestyle, climate, and natural beauty.
